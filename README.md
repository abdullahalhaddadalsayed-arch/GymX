# GymX
Database + Photo ML identifier + data analysis + progress planning + history + linked with youtube

**GymX – Your Personal AI Gym Companion**

GymX is a smart fitness app that lets you build personalized workout routines, explore exercises with pictures and videos, and track your fitness progress.

Using **AI and Machine Learning**, GymX allows you to scan gym equipment with your camera to identify machines, understand their functions, discover targeted muscles, and receive step-by-step instructions and exercise recommendations. AI also helps suggest science-based workout routines and alternative exercises based on your goals.

**Extensive Exercise Database:** Designed to integrate a library of over **1,300 exercises**, covering various muscle groups, equipment types, workout techniques, images, and demonstrations.

Track weights, reps, cardio, and workout history through interactive charts, weekly progress tracking, and body transformation photos.

**Train Smart. Track Strong.**
